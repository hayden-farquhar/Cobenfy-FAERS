## Classification reliability: pre-specified rule-based vs expert adjudication

The pre-specified rule-based classification (keyword lookup) was compared against an independent, blinded expert re-adjudication of all 64 consensus signals. The expert classification, which additionally permitted an indeterminate category, is taken as final.

- Observed agreement: 76.6%
- Cohen's kappa (three categories P/D/I): 0.57

Confusion matrix (rows = rule-based, columns = expert), categories P / D / I:

| rule-based \ expert | P | D | I |
|---|---|---|---|
| P | 31 | 6 | 8 |
| D | 0 | 18 | 1 |
| I | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Most disagreements reflect two expected sources: newer preferred terms (from the added reporting quarter) that the pre-specified rule set did not enumerate and therefore defaulted to pharmacological, and reassignment of mechanistically ambiguous terms to the indeterminate category. The expert adjudication is used throughout.
